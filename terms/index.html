Terms & Conditions

NoBacksies is not a game, contest, or wager. There are no prizes and no winnings — you can never receive more than you deposit. These terms explain exactly how the service, the deposit, and the check-in process work.

1. The service

NoBacksies is operated by Infinite Product ("we", "us", "our"), its parent company. NoBacksies is a self-commitment tool. You choose a daily habit ("Gym" or "Wake Up Early"), a duration (14, 30, or 60 days), and a refundable deposit. You place a one-time, non-refundable joining fee of ₹99 per challenge and hold a deposit of ₹500 or more for the duration you choose. If you complete the challenge, your full deposit is returned to you. If you fail to complete it, your deposit is forfeited and donated to our charity partner — it is never kept by NoBacksies.

2. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and capable of entering into a legally binding contract under Indian law to use NoBacksies. The service is intended for use within India, and payments are processed in Indian Rupees (INR).

3. Joining fee and deposit

The ₹99 joining fee is charged once per challenge, at the time you start it, and is non-refundable once the challenge has started (see our Refund & Cancellation Policy for the narrow window in which it can be refunded before that). The deposit is held separately for the duration of your challenge and is never used by NoBacksies for any purpose other than being returned to you on completion or donated on your behalf on failure.

4. Check-in requirements and verification

Each active day of a challenge requires one verified check-in. For a Gym challenge, this means a live, in-app photo capture, verified automatically to confirm it is a genuine, unedited camera capture rather than a screenshot or a photo of another screen. For Wake Up Early, this means confirming inside your chosen wake-up window — there is no photo requirement. Each challenge's schedule includes a small number of built-in rest/grace allowances per week; missing a check-in beyond that allowance counts against your challenge.

5. What constitutes failure

A challenge fails when you miss more check-ins than your plan's built-in grace allowance permits for that week. Once a challenge is marked failed, the deposit for that challenge is forfeited and scheduled for donation as described below.

6. Dispute window

If you believe a check-in was incorrectly rejected or a challenge was incorrectly marked as failed, you may raise a dispute by emailing support@nobacksies.app within 48 hours of the outcome. Forfeiture and donation processing does not begin until this window has passed.

7. Forfeit-to-charity process

A forfeited deposit is donated in full to our charity partner, Akshaya Patra, once the 48-hour dispute window has closed. (Charity registration details will be added once that partnership is finalized.) NoBacksies does not retain any portion of a forfeited deposit, and does not collect donations from users directly — the donation is made by NoBacksies, on your behalf, and a receipt is made available in the app.

We are not liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential loss arising from your use of the service, including loss arising from a missed check-in due to circumstances outside our reasonable control (device failure, connectivity loss, or similar). Our total liability to you in connection with any single challenge is limited to the amount of the deposit held for that challenge.
