Refund & Cancellation Policy

The short version: finish your challenge and your full deposit comes back. Cancel early enough and you get everything back, fee included. Fail, and the deposit is donated, not refunded.

On completion: full deposit refunded

Complete every required check-in for your challenge and your entire deposit is refunded to the original payment method used to pay it. Refunds are typically processed within 5–7 working days of completion, depending on your bank or card issuer.

The ₹99 joining fee

The joining fee is non-refundable once a challenge has started. It covers the cost of running the service — verification, hosting, and support — regardless of how the challenge ends.

Cancelling before you start

You can cancel a challenge for a full refund, including the ₹99 joining fee, if both of the following are true:

  • It has been less than 24 hours since you paid, and
  • You have not yet made your first check-in.

To cancel within this window, email support@nobacksies.app with your account email and challenge details. Once either condition above is no longer true, the challenge runs its normal course — complete it for a full deposit refund, or let it lapse and the deposit is donated.

On failure: donated, not refunded

If a challenge is marked failed — more missed check-ins than your plan's grace allowance covers — the deposit is not refunded. It is instead donated in full to our charity partner, Akshaya Patra, once the dispute window below has closed. You receive a donation receipt inside the app.

Disputing an outcome

If you believe a check-in was wrongly rejected or a challenge was wrongly marked failed, email support@nobacksies.app within 48 hours of the outcome. We hold forfeited deposits for this full window before any donation is made, specifically so a genuine dispute can still be resolved in your favor.

Contact

For anything not covered here, reach us at support@nobacksies.app or see our Contact page.
