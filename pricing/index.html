Pricing

No subscription. No hidden charges.

NoBacksies charges one flat joining fee per challenge, plus a refundable deposit you choose yourself. That's the entire pricing model.

Joining fee

₹99 per challenge, charged once when you start it. This is the only money NoBacksies ever keeps, whether you finish the challenge or not. It is non-refundable once the challenge has started — see our Refund & Cancellation Policy for the short window in which it can still be refunded before that.

Deposit

You choose an amount to deposit for the length of your challenge: ₹500, ₹1,000, or ₹2,000 are the presets in the app, or you can set any custom amount ₹500 and above. This is not a fee — it's held for you and returned in full if you complete the challenge.

No subscription, no recurring charges

There is no monthly or annual plan. Every challenge is a single, one-time transaction: the joining fee plus your chosen deposit, paid once at the start. Starting a new challenge later is a separate, independent transaction.
